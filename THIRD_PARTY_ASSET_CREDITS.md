## Generated by this project (no third-party terms)

- Daylight sky panorama `Assets/HumanEye/Generated/Textures/DaylightSky.hdr` — `Tools/HumanEye/generate_daylight_sky.py`
  (procedural sky, sun, clouds, skyline; nothing downloaded).
- Blue iris `Assets/HumanEye/ThirdParty/LocalEye/Eye_Diff_Blue.jpg` and sclera `Eye_Diff_Sclera.jpg` — derivatives of the
  owner-supplied `Eye_Diff.jpg` (`Tools/HumanEye/blue_iris.py`, `whiten_sclera.py`); they inherit that file's status (below).
- Niko, the procedural eye geometry, shaders, particles, UI, music and all synthesized sound effects
  (`generate_*_sfx.py`, `generate_music.py`); provisional narration from local Windows voices (`generate_tts.ps1`).
- The 32-second opening score `intro_discovery.wav` is original procedural synthesis from
  `Tools/HumanEye/generate_intro_music.py`, with no samples or downloaded music.
