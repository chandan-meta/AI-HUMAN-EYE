# Audio credits: Human Eye module (15 October 2026)

All music and sound effects added by the audio overhaul are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and are cleared for use in games. No attribution is required; the
authors are credited here with thanks. One row per source file used, with its processed clip and in-game use, is in
`AUDIO_SOURCES.csv`; the clips are rebuilt from the downloads by `Tools/HumanEye/build_audio_assets.py`.

Licence texts that came with the downloads are kept beside this file (`Kenney_*_License.txt`,
`CleytonKauffman_ExplorationTheme_readme.txt`). Not used: Mixkit (no clip was needed beyond the CC0 packs; Mixkit stock
music is not licensed for games), Kenney Impact Sounds (downloaded, nothing suitable for a gentle science module), and the
Dark Sci-Fi pack's "Pulse" (sub-bass tension, replaced by "Airy" from the same pack).

Still used from before the overhaul: two Kenney Interface Sounds clips (`glass_001`, `bong_001`; CC0; in
`Assets/HumanEye/ThirdParty/Kenney/Audio`, see `Docs/HumanEye/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`) and three sounds synthesised in this
project (`Tools/HumanEye/generate_ui_sfx.py`, `generate_reference_sfx.py`); their rows close `AUDIO_SOURCES.csv`.
