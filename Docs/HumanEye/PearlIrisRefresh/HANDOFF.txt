UNITY SHADER HANDOFF
Installed Unity: 6000.3.0f1. Shader: HumanEye/Anatomy.
_BaseMap: sRGB; _BumpMap: normal-map import, linear;
_SpecMap: linear red-channel specular-intensity mask.
_Gloss controls shininess as a scalar; Roughness/Smoothness are handoff maps,
not directly supported texture inputs in this shader. Do not pack them into _SpecMap.
Preserve separate retina/choroid/sclera/lens materials and vessel meshes/overlays.
Start candidate normal strength below original 0.45 and evaluate at lesson distance.
Do not assign the retinal base colour indiscriminately to choroid or sclera.
The grey/orange boundary was retained. A Unity diagnostic hiding the vitreous
removed that boundary, identifying the translucent vitreous/cutaway surface as
its cause. It was not painted into or out of the retina texture.
