# Cinematic laboratory presentation — 5 October 2026

Implemented in the saved HumanEye_Observatory scene; the ten chapters and 66 original dialogue cues are retained. Unity 6000.3.0f1, URP 17.3.0, existing Cinemachine/Input System/native Canvas. No package additions.

## Presentation and controls

- Start screen frames the existing closed gate. Start plays a 15-second approach, mechanical gate lift, entry, architectural light activation, platform accent, and human spotlight reveal. Reduced motion uses a still shot and fade. Skip cancels owned camera/fade routines and restores the interactive lighting/gate state. Replay intro restarts chapter 2 without deleting saved learning evidence.
- Scan moves toward the face, sweeps the eye region, uses a clean translucent skull view, and enlarges the same eye from its socket. Muscle and brain overlays no longer compete with the eye during this transition. The Niko pinch action and all spoken lesson lines remain.
- Explore lab becomes available while the lesson awaits Scan or free exploration. WASD/arrows or the left joystick move; right mouse drag or a viewport touch looks. Escape/Return to lesson restores the lesson camera. The CharacterController uses imported environment mesh collisions. Pauses clear held joystick input; cinematics reclaim camera control.
- Inspection retains drag-to-orbit, wheel/pinch zoom, selection/highlights, labels, and Reset view. Cross-section/Exterior and Show/Hide sclera are available during the initial anatomy explorations. Reset restores the chapter's normal layer view. UI-owned touches cannot orbit, pinch, or select anatomy through the controls.
- Sound on/off preserves individual volume settings; essential narration captions remain visible when muted. Existing settings, progress, quizzes, Ask Niko, explanations and narration are retained.
- Environment material copies lower emission and floor smoothness without modifying the owner's source materials. Peripheral lights lose shadows; the subject spotlight remains prominent. Saved procedural iris and light-convergence studies replace brain banners, and brain video playback is disabled.

Apply future changes with **Tools → Human Eye → Authoring → Apply cinematic entrance (keeps scene edits)**. This updates the listed presentation assets; it does not regenerate the module. Its collision and material changes are saved prefab-instance overrides. The existing manually edited Mt8 material is not rewritten.

Installed Blender converts it offline to four native Unity mesh assets (5,312 total triangles), aligned to the existing optical axis and normalized to the lesson radius. UV islands separate sclera, iris and pupil highlighting; the outer transparent shell is retained. The existing URP anatomy shader uses the baked base-colour texture and approximate corneal gloss/transparency; source normal/roughness maps and physical refraction are not reproduced. No runtime GLB importer or new package is needed. Cutaway transitions fade into the existing schematic internal structures. Scene 5 uses the procedural iris/pupil so its live pupil-response demonstration remains correct. The imported source does not contain the lesson's separate retina/lens/nerve structures. Reapply with Tools > Human Eye > Authoring > Apply supplied MisterH eye after running Tools/HumanEye/import_misterh_eye.py in Blender only if the source changes.

The two new banner textures are generated by CinematicPatch.cs from mathematical iris/ray illustrations. They are original project artwork, not medical photographs. Existing human/environment/audio/font notices continue to apply: see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
