## Interpretation choices (flagged, reversible)

- **Scene 2 environment:** "At the centre stands a realistic human figure… Then the AI appears." followed later by "The human figure appears." The display starts subdued (a faint silhouette), and the figure is fully revealed after S02_L03.
- **"There." / one eye illuminates:** the storyboard places "There." before "One eye illuminates". Per the brief, the illumination is timed to start with the line.
- **Scene 3 "Return to the full eye between part selections":** honoured. The choroid route always passes the retina close-up first (connecting shot) whatever order the learner chooses.
- **Scene 5 pupil response:** observed on the closed eye from the front (clearest view of pupil size). The cutaway reopens for Scene 6.
- **Scene 7 retinal image:** shown as a billboard at the convergence point plus a magnified inset ("Image on the retina: real, inverted"), because in a sagittal cut the image location lies on the cut edge. The image never travels through the nerve.
- **Scene 9 "The original object appears clearly again":** the real apple is framed, with two separate labelled views (inverted retinal image vs visual perception). There is no flipping animation.
- **Scene 11 blur:** a depth-of-field effect blurs the distant tree while the near apple stays sharp. No explanation follows the final question.
- **Optic disc** is placed 15° nasal of the posterior pole so the retinal image (on the axis) does not land on it. Proportions are illustrative and thin layers are slightly thickened for readability.
- **Audio:** no recorded narration was supplied. All 66 cues use provisional local TTS (Microsoft Mark, en-US) and must be replaced.
